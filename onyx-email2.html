A framework you can run on any supplier in this space, including us.

The 5

  1. 1

    1. Who tested it?

    A named lab you can look up on its own. Not a factory document that came with the shipment.

  2. 2

    2. Where was it tested?

    Overseas in house testing and independent US testing are not the same thing.

  3. 3

    3. Does the report match your batch?

    One generic certificate reused across products answers nothing.

  4. 4

    4. Was net content measured?

    Purity says what the powder is. Net content says how much of it you got.

  5. 5

    5. Can you reach a person?

    Before the order, not only after.
