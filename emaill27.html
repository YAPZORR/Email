What usually goes wrong

Four failure points, in order.

Where SuppliersUsually Fall Down

Four places the chain breaks. Worth checking all of them before you order from anyone.

See the Onyx Standard

1. The Lab Does Not Exist

A certificate from an unnamed lab, or one you cannot find outside the supplier’s own site, is not third party testing.

Ours is Kovera Labs, independent and US based.

2. The Report Is Not Yours

One certificate reused across every product tells you nothing about the vial you receive.

Every batch gets its own report. 80 are published.

3. Only Purity Is Tested

Purity says how much of the sample is one compound. It does not say which compound, or how much you got.

Identity and measured net content are on every report.

4. Nobody Answers After

Fulfillment and support are where most of the risk actually sits.

Domestic fulfillment from Charlotte. support@onyxbiolabs.com.
