Explore the Testing Details

The First Question

How much of this sample is one compound?

HPLC answers it. The sample is separated and the target fraction is measured. The output is a percentage.

It is the number everyone prints. It is also the easier of the 2 to produce.

The Second Question

Is that compound the one on the label?

LC-MS answers it. Molecular mass is measured and the sequence is confirmed against a reference standard.

A supplier that runs only the first test can tell you their material is clean without being able to tell you what it is.
